    How the meta-score is computed

    1. Every synthetic dataset (method × seed) is ranked against all the others on each metric: rank 1 is the highest score, or the lowest risk for the privacy metrics. Ties share the lowest rank.
    2. The rank-derived score of a method on a metric is the mean rank of its datasets.
    3. A pillar score is the mean of the rank-derived scores of the pillar's metrics.
    4. The meta-score is the weighted sum of the pillar scores (weights summing to 1). Lower is better.

    Ranks compress the size of the differences: read the metric values alongside the ranking.
